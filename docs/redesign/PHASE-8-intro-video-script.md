# BaroPlatform intro video: script and storyboard

Length: about 75 seconds. Format: screen recording of the real app with a voice-over (or on-screen captions),
1920×1080, MP4 (H.264) or WebM. Keep the file under 100 MB to upload it in the admin, or upload it to YouTube as
an *unlisted* video and paste the link in Site Content → Home page → Intro video.

The four scenes match the storyboard preview that plays on the site until the real video is added.

## Recording checklist

- Use a test account with a finished lecture ready, so you do not wait on processing while recording.
- Hide notifications and bookmarks. Use the light theme, and 125% browser zoom so text is readable on phones.
- Record in one take per scene and join them. Add captions in English and Somali if you can.
- Export MP4 (H.264, AAC audio), 1080p, about 5 to 8 Mbps. A 75-second video at that rate is roughly 50 to 75 MB.
- Make a cover image (a screenshot of scene 3 works well) at 1600×700 for the card.

## Putting it on the site

1. Sign in to the admin and open **Site Content → Home page → Intro video**.
2. Either upload the video file, or paste the unlisted YouTube link.
3. Optionally upload the cover image, then press **Save and publish**.
4. Open the home page, press **Play intro**, and check it plays on desktop and on a phone.
5. The same video appears on the About page. Switch that off in **About page → Show the intro video** if you do not want it there.
